Eden AI

Verified Checked 2026-10-25

An AI gateway that routes to many LLM and specialist-model vendors (OCR, speech, vision, translation) through one OpenAI-compatible endpoint, key and credit balance. Scope is AI models rather than general data APIs.

How it bills
Pay-as-you-go from prepaid credits with no subscription, 7 requests/second by default (upgradable to 15 on request) and auto-refill available; every API response returns the exact USD cost. A sandbox token allows free testing. An Advanced AI Platform tier with custom pricing and bulk discounts is sold by sales.
Payment methods
card (Stripe), bank transfer, credits (prepaid)
Fees stated
Eden AI charges no markup on provider pricing. You pay exactly what the underlying provider charges, plus a 5.5% platform fee applied at checkout.
Rate limits
