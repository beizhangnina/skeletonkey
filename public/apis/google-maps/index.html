When to pick which

  • Direct signup requires a card; the Glasser route needs only google, github, email sign-in and prepaid credit.
  • Only the direct route exposes all endpoints; Glasser exposes 1 endpoint.
  • Listed prices: direct $0.002 per call; Glasser $0.0005 per call; Apify Store from $1.50 / 1,000 scraped places.
  • The Apify Store route can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser lists Google Maps under Local & places with a single endpoint, Google Maps Contributor Reviews, at $0.0005 per call. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
Apify's own heavily used Google Maps Scraper (about 666k users) shows pay-per-result pricing from $1.50 per 1,000 scraped places on its store page. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.

Listed in: Glasser.
