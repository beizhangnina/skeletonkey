When to pick which

  • Neither the direct signup nor the Monid and Apify Store routes ask for a card.
  • Only the direct route exposes all endpoints; Monid exposes 10 endpoints.
  • Listed prices: Monid $0.0100 per call; Apify Store from $10.00 / 1,000 results.
  • The Monid and Apify Store routes can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Monid
The rendered page lists ten Weather Underground endpoints, nine at $0.0100 per call (including /get_monthly_observations) and one at $0.0200. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
Third-party Actor (about 69 users) scraping Weather Underground forecasts and history; the store page shows pay-per-result pricing from $10.00 per 1,000 results. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.

Listed in: Monid.
