When to pick which

  • Neither the direct signup nor the Glasser, Monid and Apify Store routes ask for a card.
  • Only the direct route exposes all endpoints; Glasser exposes 210 endpoints.
  • Listed prices: direct $0.00015 per call; Glasser $0.00 per call; Apify Store from $2.40 / 1,000 keyword results; RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub) Basic $0.00 /mo (500,000 requests per month).
  • The Monid route is listed unverified: no price was readable there on 2026-10-25.

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser's DataForSEO page listed 210 published endpoints across 11 families; 36 of them show $0.00 per call, including the historical SERPs endpoint (max $0.0018 per run). Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Monid
The Monid page for Dataforseo loads but says 'No endpoints here yet', and its guide quotes no DataForSEO prices, so I could not confirm any price. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
A DataForSEO Labs keyword actor on Apify Store (608 users) showed $2.40 per 1,000 keyword results. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub)
DataForSEO's own RapidAPI org lists DataForSEO Labs, whose pricing tab showed a single Basic plan at $0.00/mo with a 500k request hard limit. Free credit or trial: Per-API recurring monthly subscription plans (many with a free BASIC tier) plus overage fees per call above a plan's quota

Listed in: Glasser, Monid.
