Apple Maps

Unverified Checked 2026-10-25

Apple's Maps Server API: a REST service for geocoding and reverse geocoding, place search and autocomplete, directions and ETAs, and place/place-photo lookups, intended to be called from your server instead of from MapKit on-device.

Route notes

Glasser
Provider page listed 2 published endpoints, both at $0.0005 per call, the cheapest shown being /places. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
Apify Actor page for Apple Maps Scraper Pro showed a pricing badge of from $3.29 / 1,000 places. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
