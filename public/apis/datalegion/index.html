Data Legion

Verified Checked 2026-10-25

B2B person and company data API: match a person or company by email, name, domain, LinkedIn or ticker and get back a profile with base, premium and (for people) contact fields; also offers search/discovery endpoints, an MCP server, CLI and monthly bulk data feeds.

When to pick which

  • Neither the direct signup nor the Monid and Apify Store routes ask for a card.
  • Listed prices: direct $0.01 per call; Apify Store from $10.00 / 1,000 person base matches.
  • The Monid route is listed unverified: no price was readable there on 2026-10-25.
  • The Monid and Apify Store routes can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Monid
Monid's datalegion page is live but its catalog block reads 'No endpoints here yet' and every priced row in its guides belongs to other providers (apollo, pdl, akta, clay), so no Data Legion endpoint or price appears. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
Data Legion's own Apify account publishes this enrichment Actor, priced 'from $10.00 / 1,000 person base matches' with a per-match table starting at $0.01. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.

Listed in: Monid.
