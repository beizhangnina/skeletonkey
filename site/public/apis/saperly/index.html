Saperly

Verified Checked 2026-10-26

Saperly is a telephony layer for AI agents: one API call provisions a real phone number and binds it to a reusable 'connection' that either runs a hosted voice agent or relays turns to your own runtime, with voice, SMS, 10DLC/consent compliance and an audit trail handled by Saperly.

Register locally with SkeletonKey

This page does not collect your key. Install SkeletonKey on your own machine (setup: npx -y @dormy/skeletonkey init), then let your agent find and register Saperly for you: it signs up on the vendor's own pages under your own account, verification codes and payment stay with you, and the key it gets back is saved to your own local vault — never shown to an AI assistant, and never sent to this site.
