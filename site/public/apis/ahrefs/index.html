Route notes

Glasser
Provider page said 38 endpoints and listed /v3/public/domain-rating-top-domains at $0.0005 per call as the cheapest shown. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Monid
Monid's catalog table for this provider read "No endpoints here yet", so no endpoint count was shown; the cheapest Ahrefs row shown was "/site-explorer/pages-by-internal-links $0.001935 / result", and no /site-explorer/domain-rating-history or $0.00129 price appeared. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
Apify Actor page for the Ahrefs All-in-One SEO Scraper showed a pricing badge of from $4.00 / 1,000 results. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
