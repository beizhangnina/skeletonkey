Register locally with SkeletonKey

This page does not collect your key. Install SkeletonKey on your own machine (setup: npx -y @dormy/skeletonkey init), then let your agent find and register OpenAI API for you: it signs up on the vendor's own pages under your own account, verification codes and payment stay with you, and the key it gets back is saved to your own local vault — never shown to an AI assistant, and never sent to this site.

Other routes (reference only)

For comparison: besides registering your own account (see Register locally above), this data has also shown up through aggregator gateways, each billed through that gateway's own key, not yours. SkeletonKey does not recommend routing your calls through a gateway key instead of your own account. Every figure here is ours, read on the checked date; unverified routes carry no confirmed price.

What differs between them

  • The Monid route can be paid per call in USDC over x402.
  • The Monid route starts new accounts with free credit.
  • The direct route needs your own vendor key; the Monid route uses the aggregator's key.
