Route notes

Monid
Monid's elevenlabs page says 'No endpoints here yet' in its catalog block, yet two guide tables on it run 'elevenlabs /voices' at $0.00 alongside paid endpoints like /v1/music at $0.15 per minute. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
This Apify Actor wrapping ElevenLabs text-to-speech (65 users) prices at 'from $5.00 / 1,000 elevenlabs speech syntheses' on its pricing tab. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
