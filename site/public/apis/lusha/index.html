Lusha

Updated 2026-10-25

B2B contact and company data: search people or accounts by name, domain, email or LinkedIn URL, then spend credits to reveal verified work emails and phone numbers, firmographics, buying-intent signals and website-visitor identification. Delivered via REST API, MCP server, browser extension and CRM integrations.

API surface

Official docs
https://docs.lusha.com/api-reference/overview
EndpointDoesReturnsDocs
POST /v3/contacts/searchSearch for contacts by identifiers such as name plus company domain, LinkedIn URL, or email; accepts batches and echoes back clientReferenceId.Matching contacts and what data is available on each; revealing emails and phones requires a separate Enrich call.Docs
GET /v3/account/usageReturns a single snapshot of account status: credit balance, rate-limit usage per window, plan details, and per-action credit pricing.JSON with credits (total/used/remaining), rateLimits (daily/hourly/minute), plan, and pricing map of action to credits.Docs
POST /v3/companies/tablesCreates a new, empty companies table, optionally seeded with an initial list of company IDs. Billing is free.Not described in the provided text beyond creating the table (free per billing note).Docs
GET /v3/companies/tables/{table_id}Retrieves a companies table's metadata and its current processing status. Billing is free.Table metadata and current processing status.Docs
GET /v3/contacts/tables/columnsLists every Lusha data column that can be added to a contacts table (22 columns), with no request body or parameters.Column entries with category, displayName, columnKey, and type; free, no credits charged.Docs
