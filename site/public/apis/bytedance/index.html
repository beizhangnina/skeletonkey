BytePlus (ModelArk)

Verified Checked 2026-10-26

BytePlus ModelArk is ByteDance's international model-serving platform: a single REST/OpenAI-style API for ByteDance Seed LLMs, Dreamina Seedance video generation, Dola Seedream image generation, 3D generation, embeddings, speech and managed agents.

When to pick which

  • The Monid and Apify Store routes start new accounts with free credit.

Route notes

Monid
Provider page is live on Monid but its endpoint catalog section reads "No endpoints here yet"; the price I quote comes from the per-endpoint cost rows inside the how-to guide on that same page. Only this one Seedance endpoint is priced on the page; no seedance-2.0-mini or $3.50 figure appears. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
Actor runs ByteDance Seedance models exclusively, per-second rates $0.08-$0.90; it is third-party with very few users. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.

Listed in: Monid.
