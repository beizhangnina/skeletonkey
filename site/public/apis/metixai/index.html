Metix AI

Verified Checked 2026-10-25

Searches people, jobs, and companies and retrieves their records or contact details through a REST API and MCP server.

EndpointDoesReturnsDocs
POST /v1/people-searchNatural-language people search; returns encrypted profile IDs only, which must then be sent to the profile detail route to get records.Encrypted profile IDs (e.g. profile_ids array), not record data.Docs
POST /v1/people/queryQuery Spec (structured) search over the people dataset; returns encrypted IDs for a second detail call.Encrypted string IDs plus a paging cursor while more pages remain.Docs
POST /entity/v1/profiles/detail-by-idFetch full profile records for up to 100 profile IDs obtained from a people search.Full public profile records and the IDs that could not be resolved.Docs
POST /v1/companies/queryQuery Spec search over organizations, used to build account lists and attach company context to people and jobs.Encrypted string company IDs, not company records.Docs
POST /entity/v1/companies/detail-by-idFetch company records, including headquarters, funding round and stock listings, for up to 100 company IDs.Company record fields such as name, size, industry, headquarters, funding and listings.Docs
