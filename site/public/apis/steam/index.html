Steam

Verified Checked 2026-10-25

Valve's Steam Web API: HTTP endpoints that return Steam data as JSON/XML/VDF - app news, global achievement and game stats, player summaries and owned games, inventory/economy items, plus publisher-only interfaces for microtransactions, sales data and ownership checks.

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser lists Steam with one published endpoint, Steam App Detail, at $0.0005 per call. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub)
A third-party (not Valve) RapidAPI listing of Steam store data whose pricing tab shows a free Basic plan of 300 store calls a day with $0.10 per extra call, then Pro at $5/mo. Free credit or trial: Per-API recurring monthly subscription plans (many with a free BASIC tier) plus overage fees per call above a plan's quota
