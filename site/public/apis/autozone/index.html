AutoZone

No public API Checked 2026-10-26

AutoZone's consumer e-commerce site for automotive parts and accessories: vehicle-fit part search, store/stock lookup, online ordering and in-store pickup. It publishes no developer API.

Apify Store
This AutoZone Products Scraper actor page on Apify Store (31 users, ~5.6k runs) showed $0.80 per 1,000 results. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
