Apify Store
A small unofficial Apify Actor wraps DeepSeek chat completions and charges $6.00 per 1,000 replies according to its store page. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
