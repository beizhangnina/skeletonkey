What differs between them

  • No self-serve direct route (sales only); the Glasser, Monid and Apify Store routes are self-serve.
  • Glasser exposes 3 endpoints; the vendor publishes no self-serve API we could verify.
  • Listed prices: Glasser $0.011 per call; Monid $0.03; Apify Store from $0.50 / 1,000 results.
  • The Monid and Apify Store routes can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser showed 3 Trustpilot endpoints, the categories and companies-by-category calls at $0.011 per call and the review summary at $0.055. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Monid
Monid's Trustpilot page listed no endpoint catalog, but its how-to priced trustpilot /get_company_overview and /get_company_reviews at $0.03 and the review summary at $0.05; the $0.01 user-profile endpoint from the catalog was not shown. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
A popular Apify Trustpilot reviews Actor shows $0.50 per 1,000 results, plus optional AI analysis events priced per company. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
