What differs between them

  • Only the direct route exposes all endpoints; Glasser exposes 1 endpoint.
  • Listed prices: Glasser $0.0077 per call; Apify Store from $3.20 / 1,000 jobs.
  • The Apify Store route can be paid per call in USDC over x402.
  • The Glasser and Apify Store routes start new accounts with free credit.

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser's Upwork page has one published endpoint, Upwork Job Search, at $0.0077 per call. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
The most-used Upwork Actor on Apify (7,256 users, 4.8 rating) shows 'from $3.20 / 1,000 jobs' on its pricing tab. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
