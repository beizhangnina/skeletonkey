What differs between them

  • Direct signup requires a card; the Glasser route needs only google, github, email sign-in and prepaid credit.
  • Only the direct route exposes all endpoints; Glasser exposes 3 endpoints.
  • Listed prices: direct $0.001 per call; Glasser $0.0005 per result; Apify Store from $0.40 / 1,000 tweets; RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub) Basic $1.00 /mo — Requests 1,000 / Month.
  • The Apify Store route can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser's X page listed 3 endpoints and the verified-followers one was the cheapest at $0.0005 per result. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
Apify's dominant X/Twitter Actor (over 169M runs) shows a headline price of $0.40 per thousand tweets. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub)
The Twttr API pricing tab starts at $1.00/mo for 1,000 requests, with Pro at $25.00/mo. Free credit or trial: Per-API recurring monthly subscription plans (many with a free BASIC tier) plus overage fees per call above a plan's quota
