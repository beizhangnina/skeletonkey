API index / people-company-data

akta.pro

Verified Checked 2026-10-25

Free / pay as you go

Private-company data and news-signal APIs for agents: company enrichment across 20M+ entities with 75+ data points sold per section, entity-resolved news with 80+ event signals, filter- or natural-language list generation, and alternative signals such as headcount trends, web traffic, job posts and reviews.

API surface

Official docs
https://docs.akta.pro/
EndpointDoesReturnsDocs
GET /api/v1/newsFetch news articles enriched with AI summaries, sentiment, event types, publisher metadata, industry tags, and company mentions; supports filters and pagination.JSON with total, limit, offset, count and a data array of enriched article objects including full_text, entities, and scores.Docs
GET /v1/company/employee-reviewsFetch a company's employee review data: overall rating, dimension-level ratings per provider, and a paginated list of individual reviews.JSON with data.employee_reviews (overall_rating, other_ratings, reviews) plus limit, offset, and credits_consumed.Docs
POST /api/v1/list/filter-builder/Converts a natural language description of target companies into the structured filters object accepted by the List Generation API.JSON with a data.filters object of dotted-path filter keys and a credits_consumed value; no companies are returned.Docs
GET /api/v1/company/search/Resolve a company from a free-text query; the overview page uses it as the first request to try.JSON with a data array of companies, each with name, website, company_status, and product_category.Docs
GET /api/v1/company/enrichment/Enrich a company by UUID or website, requesting one or more data sections such as firmographic.JSON with data.uuid, the requested section objects (e.g. firmographic), and credits_consumed.Docs

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser lists 12 Akta endpoints; the two resolver endpoints (company identity, industry taxonomy) are $0.00 per call and the rest run from $0.005 up to $0.33. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Monid
Monid's akta page shows an empty catalog, but its guide tables price three akta endpoints, the cheapest being /v1/news at $0.0005 per result; the $0 /v1/industry/search rate was not visible anywhere on the page. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
