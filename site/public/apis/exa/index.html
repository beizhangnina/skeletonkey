Route notes

Glasser
Eight Exa endpoints are listed; the contents/body-text one is the cheapest at $0.0011 per result with a $0.05 per-run cap, against $0.004-$0.0165 per call for the search and answer routes. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Monid
Only two Exa endpoints on the page: /contents at $0.0010 per call and /search at "from $0.0070 per call". Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
