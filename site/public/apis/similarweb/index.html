What differs between them

  • No self-serve direct route (sales only); the Glasser and Apify Store routes are self-serve.
  • Glasser exposes 7 endpoints; the vendor publishes no self-serve API we could verify.
  • Listed prices: Glasser $0.0825 per result, max $4.95 per run; Apify Store from $13.00 / 1,000 results.
  • The Apify Store route can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Glasser
Six of the 7 Similarweb endpoints on Glasser are $0.0825 (per result or per call) and one age-group endpoint is $0.495, so $0.0825 per result is the floor. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
The most-used Similarweb Actor on Apify (4,678 users, 4.6 rating) prices at 'from $13.00 / 1,000 results'. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
