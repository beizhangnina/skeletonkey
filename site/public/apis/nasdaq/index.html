Route notes

Glasser
Provider page listed 5 published endpoints, all at $0.011 per call, the cheapest shown being Nasdaq Dividend History (/get_stock_dividend_history). Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Monid
Monid's catalog table for this provider read "No endpoints here yet", so no endpoint count was shown; the guide showed "nasdaq /get_stock_earnings $0.01, and every Nasdaq row on the page was $0.01. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
Apify Actor page for the Nasdaq Short Interest Scraper showed a pricing badge of from $6.00 / 1,000 results. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
