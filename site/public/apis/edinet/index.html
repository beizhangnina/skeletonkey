EDINET

Verified Checked 2026-10-26

Cost not verified

EDINET is the Japanese Financial Services Agency's statutory disclosure system. Its v2 REST API lists the documents filed on a given day (metadata JSON) and downloads a chosen filing as XBRL, PDF, an attachment archive or English/CSV data.

Route notes

Glasser
One published endpoint, a by-date securities-filings lookup, at $0.0005 per call. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
Actor that reads the official EDINET API; its Pricing block showed the per-1,000-records rate. Small install base (2 users, ~104 runs). Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
