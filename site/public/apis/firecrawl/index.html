What differs between them

  • Neither the direct signup nor the Glasser, Monid and Apify Store routes ask for a card.
  • Only the direct route exposes all endpoints; Glasser exposes 5 endpoints.
  • Listed prices: direct $0.000749 per call; Glasser $0.0002 per result, max $0.06 per run; Monid $0.001 to $0.004; Apify Store from $0.01 / 1,000 results.
  • The Monid and Apify Store routes can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser lists 5 Firecrawl endpoints; the search endpoint is the cheapest at $0.0002 per result capped at $0.06 per run, the other four being $0.001. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Monid
Monid's firecrawl page has an empty catalog block, but its guide tables run 'firecrawl /scrape' and '/crawl' at a banded '$0.001 to $0.004'; the $0.0002 search rate was not shown. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
This Firecrawl-powered crawler Actor (119 users) shows 'from $0.01 / 1,000 results' plus a $0.05 per-event price in its README table. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
