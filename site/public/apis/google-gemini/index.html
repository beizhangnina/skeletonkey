Register locally with SkeletonKey

This page does not collect your key. Install SkeletonKey on your own machine (setup: npx skeletonkey init), then let your agent find and register Gemini API for you: it signs up on the vendor's own pages under your own account, verification codes and payment stay with you, and the key it gets back is saved to your own local vault — never shown to an AI assistant, and never sent to this site.

Route notes

Apify Store
Apify Actor page for the official Apify Gemini Search Scraper showed a pricing badge of from $3.00 / 1,000 gemini searches. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
