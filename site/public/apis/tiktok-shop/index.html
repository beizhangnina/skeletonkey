TikTok Shop Partner Center

Updated 2026-10-26

TikTok Shop's developer platform (Partner Center): register as a seller developer or ISV, create an app, obtain shop/seller authorization, then call the TTS Open API for products, orders, fulfillment, logistics, returns, finance, affiliate and analytics data, plus webhooks.

What differs between them

  • Neither the direct signup nor the Glasser and Apify Store routes ask for a card.
  • Only the direct route exposes all endpoints; Glasser exposes 1 endpoint.
  • Listed prices: direct $0 per call; Glasser $0.00605 per call; Apify Store from $9.00 / 1,000 per results; RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub) Basic $0.00 /mo - Requests 25 / Month.
  • The Apify Store route can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Glasser
Single published endpoint on the page, priced at $0.00605 per call. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
TikTok Shop search Actor with 2,500 users and ~110k runs; the per-1,000 rate was shown in its Pricing block. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub)
TikTok Shop Scraper API listing; pricing tab showed a free Basic tier of 25 requests and a $10.00/mo Pro tier of 3,000 plus $0.003 overage. Free credit or trial: Per-API recurring monthly subscription plans (many with a free BASIC tier) plus overage fees per call above a plan's quota
