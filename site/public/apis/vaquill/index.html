Self-serve API over US primary law - statutes, regulations, constitutions, court rules, executive orders and agency material - with citation resolution, hybrid search, full text and a link to the official source on every section.

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser's Vaquill page listed 13 endpoints; Statute Coverage (/us/statutes/coverage) was the cheapest at $0.0005 per call, the rest $0.011-$0.066. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Monid
Monid's endpoint catalog section for this provider reads 'No endpoints here yet', but its how-to guide on the same page lists eight Vaquill endpoints, with /us/statutes/coverage at $0.00 and the others $0.01-$0.06. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
Vaquill's own Apify account publishes a US Primary Law actor, priced $15.00 per 1,000 Vaquill data credits on its store page. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
