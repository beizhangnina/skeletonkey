Route notes

Glasser
Four endpoints listed; item comments was the cheapest of the three price points shown ($0.016236 / $0.024354 / $0.032472 per call). Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
Most-installed Douyin Shop (抖音商城) product Actor found (4,871 users, 5.0 rating); the per-1,000-products rate was in its Pricing block. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub)
Douyin/China TikTok listing covering product detail and shop product list; pricing tab showed a free Basic tier of 20 requests and a $19.99/mo Pro tier of 2,000. Free credit or trial: Per-API recurring monthly subscription plans (many with a free BASIC tier) plus overage fees per call above a plan's quota
