What differs between them

  • Neither the direct signup nor the Glasser and Apify Store routes ask for a card.
  • Only the direct route exposes all endpoints; Glasser exposes 1 endpoint.
  • Listed prices: direct $0 per call; Glasser $0.00165 per call; Apify Store from $0.10 / 1,000 results; RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub) Basic $0.00 /mo - Requests 0 / Month + $0.
  • The Apify Store route can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Glasser
One published endpoint, a Hacker News search call, at $0.00165 per call. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
Most-installed Hacker News Actor found (218 users, ~7.4k runs); per-1,000-results rate shown in its Pricing block. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub)
RapidAPI community Hacker News listing; its pricing tab showed only a free Basic tier with no per-request charge. Free credit or trial: Per-API recurring monthly subscription plans (many with a free BASIC tier) plus overage fees per call above a plan's quota
