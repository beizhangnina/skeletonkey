WeChat

Unverified Checked 2026-10-26

Cost not verified

Tencent's WeChat developer platform exposes server-side HTTP APIs for Official/Service Accounts, Mini Programs and Mini Games, Open Platform (UnionID, WeChat Login for mobile/web apps), WeChat Store, Channels, WeChat Pay and AI/OCR services - all called against api.weixin.qq.com with an access_token minted from an app's AppID and AppSecret.

Other checks
Account registration requires picking an entity type (government / media / enterprise / other organisation / individual) and verifying it against the business licence, plus an administrator scanning a QR code in the WeChat app; Open Platform mobile/website apps additionally go through Tencent review before an AppID is issued.

Route notes

Glasser
Nine published endpoints across three price points; channels creator detail was the cheapest at $0.048708 per call. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
Most-installed WeChat Official Account Actor (4,009 users, 5.0 rating, Rising star badge); per-1,000-articles rate shown in its Pricing block. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub)
Weixin/WeChat Official Accounts Platform listing; pricing tab showed a free Basic tier with small monthly caps and a $39.99/mo Pro tier. Free credit or trial: Per-API recurring monthly subscription plans (many with a free BASIC tier) plus overage fees per call above a plan's quota
