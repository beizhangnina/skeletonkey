cloro

Verified Checked 2026-10-25

Free / pay as you go

API that returns structured results from Google Search/News and from AI answer surfaces (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini, Google AI Mode, AI Overview), including sources, citation pills, shopping cards and ads, via sync, async or batch requests.

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser lists cloro under AI search with one endpoint, Google Gemini App Answer, at $0.00088 per result and a $0.00704 per-run ceiling. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Monid
The rendered page lists seven cloro engines (/google/search, /chatgpt/ask, /gemini/ask, /copilot/ask, /perplexity/ask, /google/ai-mode, /google/news), every one at $0.008 per 10 results. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
