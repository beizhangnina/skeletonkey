Route notes

Glasser
Of the 12 BuiltWith endpoints on the page only two prices appear, $0.0005 and $0.05445; the trends counter /trends/v6/api.json is one of the $0.0005 ones and its blurb calls it free. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
Published under BuiltWith's own Apify account (about 722 users); the store page shows a flat $2.00 per 1,000 results with no cheaper tier. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub)
Listed by BuiltWith Pty Ltd itself; the pricing tab's free Basic plan gives 50 domain lookups a month and charges $0.0495 per request beyond that, the same rate Pro ($99/mo) uses for overage. Free credit or trial: Per-API recurring monthly subscription plans (many with a free BASIC tier) plus overage fees per call above a plan's quota
