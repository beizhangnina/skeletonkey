Litescrape

Verified Checked 2026-10-26

SERP and web-scraping API that returns structured JSON for Google/Bing/DuckDuckGo/Baidu/Yandex/Brave search, maps and places, reviews, app stores, marketplaces and social sites, plus alpha web fetch and screenshot endpoints (188 documented APIs).

Route notes

Monid
Monid's Litescrape page published no endpoint table, but its examples priced nine litescrape routes including /duckduckgo/maps at $0.00015 per call. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
Litescrape publishes its own Actors on Apify; this one's store page shows $0.25 per 1,000 successful requests, the same rate as its sibling Actors. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
