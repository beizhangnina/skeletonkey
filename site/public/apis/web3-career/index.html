What differs between them

  • Neither the direct signup nor the Monid and Apify Store routes ask for a card.

  • The Monid route is listed unverified: no price was readable there on 2026-10-25.
  • The Monid and Apify Store routes can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Monid
A Monid tool page for Web3 Career does exist and returns 200, but it lists no endpoints and shows no price for this provider. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
An Apify Actor dedicated to web3.career job listings shows $3.00 per 1,000 job results, broken down on the page as $0.003 per result plus $0.001 per run. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
