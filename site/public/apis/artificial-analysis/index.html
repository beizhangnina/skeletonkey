Route notes

Monid's endpoint panel for this page showed nothing, but the page's own how-to section listed artificial-analysis endpoints with per-call prices, the cheapest being $0.01 for /search_models, /get_model_detail, /get_model_providers and /get_llm_providers_leaderboard. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
