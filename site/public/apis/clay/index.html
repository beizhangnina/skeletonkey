Route notes

Monid
Monid's endpoint catalog section for this provider reads 'No endpoints here yet', but its how-to guide on the same page lists two Clay enrichment endpoints, /enrichment/company-employee-count and /enrichment/company-job-openings, both $0.032 per call; I saw no $0 Clay entry and no /search/query-mode. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
