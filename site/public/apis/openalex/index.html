OpenAlex

Verified Checked 2026-10-25

Open catalog of scholarly research - works, authors, institutions, sources, topics, funders - exposed as a free REST API you can filter, search, group and page over, plus cached open-access full texts and an OQL analytics endpoint.

What differs between them

Route notes

Glasser
Provider page listed 9 published endpoints with five at $0.0005 per call; no bare /works path is shown anywhere, and the text-search endpoint is dearer at $0.0011 per call. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
Apify Actor page for the OpenAlex Scholarly Works Scraper showed a pricing badge of $4.00 / 1,000 openalex research rows. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
