The Companies API

Verified Checked 2026-10-25

Company enrichment and search API: turn a domain, email or social URL into a company profile with 300+ data points, search ~50M companies by 50+ criteria or natural-language prompt, find similar companies, and ask AI questions about a company or fetch its text context.
