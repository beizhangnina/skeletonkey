What differs between them

  • Only the direct route exposes all endpoints; Glasser exposes 2 endpoints.
  • Listed prices: Glasser $0.024354 per call; Apify Store from $7.99 / 1,000 products; RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub) Basic $0.00 /mo (50 advanced requests, 100 requests per month).
  • The Apify Store route can be paid per call in USDC over x402.
  • The Glasser, Apify Store and RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub) routes start new accounts with free credit.

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser's Taobao page listed two endpoints, Item Questions (/item-questions) and Item Reviews, both at $0.024354 per call. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
This Taobao/Tmall product detail actor (6,516 users) showed $7.99 per 1,000 products on its Apify Store page. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub)
The Taobao DataHub pricing tab on RapidAPI showed a free Basic plan at $0.00/mo with 100 requests a month, then Pro at $29.99/mo. Free credit or trial: Per-API recurring monthly subscription plans (many with a free BASIC tier) plus overage fees per call above a plan's quota
