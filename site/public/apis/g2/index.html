What differs between them

  • Neither the direct signup nor the Glasser, Monid and Apify Store routes ask for a card.
  • Only the direct route exposes all endpoints; Glasser exposes 3 endpoints.
  • Listed prices: Glasser $0.011 per call; Monid $0.01; Apify Store from $0.50 / 1,000 rows.
  • The Monid and Apify Store routes can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser's G2 page listed 3 endpoints: Product Overview $0.011, Product Pricing $0.022 and Software Categories $0.055 per call. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Monid
Monid's G2 page showed no endpoint table, but its review walkthroughs quoted g2 /get_product_overview at $0.01, the cheapest g2 price on the page. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
A very popular G2 Actor (over 430k runs) priced from $0.50 per thousand rows, with a free monthly review allowance advertised. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
