Route notes

Glasser
Glasser's Semrush page listed 21 endpoints; the cheapest tier, including the all-database domain overview, was $0.00055 per result. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
A popular Semrush-data Actor (about 153k runs) priced from $4.00 per thousand results. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
RapidAPI (Nokia API Hub)
This Semrush keyword listing's pricing tab shows a free Basic plan of 100 requests a month, though the plan notes say the publisher has moved to a newer API. Free credit or trial: Per-API recurring monthly subscription plans (many with a free BASIC tier) plus overage fees per call above a plan's quota
