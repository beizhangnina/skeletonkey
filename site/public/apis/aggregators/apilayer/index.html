APILayer

Updated 2026-10-25

A first-party suite of data APIs (aviation, finance, geolocation, email validation, news, weather, scraping) sold under one account and one key, rather than a third-party reseller marketplace. All products share a single base URL pattern and the same query-parameter auth.

Before you route through this

SkeletonKey lists aggregator gateways as directory entries, not as a recommended access layer. A gateway holds one key that stands in for many vendors; keeping your own account and key with each vendor keeps you in control of your own data, limits and billing. Register locally with SkeletonKey instead where you can — the details below are what we read on this gateway's own pages, for reference.
