OpenRouter

Verified Checked 2026-10-25

An LLM router that fronts hundreds of models from many inference providers behind one OpenAI-compatible endpoint and key, adding fallbacks, routing and cost tracking. Models only, no third-party data APIs.

How it bills
Pay-as-you-go per token from prepaid credits. Models with the :free variant cost nothing but are rate limited per day, and buying credits raises the limit. Bring-your-own-key usage above the free allowance is charged a percentage of the equivalent OpenRouter cost.
