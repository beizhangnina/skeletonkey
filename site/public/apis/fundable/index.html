What differs between them

  • Neither the direct signup nor the Glasser and Monid routes ask for a card.
  • Only the direct route exposes all endpoints; Glasser exposes 7 endpoints.
  • Listed prices: direct $0.05 per call; Glasser $0.0005 per call; Monid $0.01.
  • The Monid route can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser showed 7 Fundable endpoints, with the industry and location lookups cheapest at $0.0005 per call. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Monid
Monid's Fundable page showed no endpoint catalog, but its how-to section priced fundable /company/search at $0.01 and /company at $0.06; the free /location/search endpoint from the catalog was not visible. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
