Finviz

No public API Updated 2026-10-25

Stock-market research site: screener, charts, heat maps, news, insider and fundamental data. The Elite subscription adds real-time quotes, advanced screening and CSV/Excel export of screener and financial data.

What differs between them

  • No self-serve direct route (no public API on the vendor pages); the Glasser, Monid and Apify Store routes are self-serve.
  • Glasser exposes 1 endpoint; the vendor publishes no self-serve API we could confirm.
  • Listed prices: Glasser $0.011 per call; Monid $0.01; Apify Store from $20.00 / 1,000 results.
  • The Monid and Apify Store routes can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Direct
No public self-serve API on the vendor's own pages.
Glasser
Finviz page shows exactly one published endpoint at $0.011 per call. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Monid
Provider page is live on Monid but its endpoint catalog section reads "No endpoints here yet"; the price I quote comes from the per-endpoint cost rows inside the how-to guide on that same page. Screener, insider-trading and news-sentiment endpoints all appear at $0.01. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
Actor page headline reads from $20.00 per 1,000 results while its pricing section states $10.00 per 1,000 results ($0.01 per stock) plus a per-start fee. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
