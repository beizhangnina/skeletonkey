Keepa

Verified Checked 2026-10-25

Returns Amazon catalog data as JSON: price/sales-rank/offer history back to 2011, product lookups by ASIN, deals, best-seller and category lists, and seller data across 11 Amazon marketplaces. The same key also powers Keepa's hosted MCP server for AI assistants.

What differs between them

  • Direct signup requires a card; the Glasser route needs only google, github, email sign-in and prepaid credit.
  • Only the direct route exposes all endpoints; Glasser exposes 14 endpoints.
  • Listed prices: Glasser $0.00144 per call; Apify Store from $4.00 / 1,000 product results.
  • The Apify Store route can be paid per call in USDC over x402.

Route notes

Glasser
Glasser's Keepa page listed 14 endpoints today and the lowest of the per-call rates shown was $0.00144, on the categories, lightning-deal, seller-info and category-name lookups. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
The Apify Store listing for this Keepa Actor (93 users) shows 'from $4.00 / 1,000 product results' on its pricing tab. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
