What differs between them

  • Listed prices: Monid $0.0035 / result; Apify Store from $40.00 / 1,000 480p video seconds.
  • The Monid and Apify Store routes can be paid per call in USDC over x402.
  • The Monid and Apify Store routes start new accounts with free credit.
  • The direct route needs your own vendor key; the Monid route uses the aggregator's key.

Route notes

Monid
Monid's endpoint catalog section for this provider reads 'No endpoints here yet', but its how-to guide on the same page lists six MiniMax endpoints; the cheapest I saw was /v1/image_generation at $0.0035 per result, with video models from $0.025/second and no free or $0 MiniMax entry. Free credit or trial: New accounts start with $1 in free credit.
Apify Store
This MiniMax Hailuo H3 video generator actor (278 users) showed $40.00 per 1,000 480p video seconds on its Apify Store page. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
