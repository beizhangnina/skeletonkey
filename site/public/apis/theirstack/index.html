Route notes

Glasser
Single TheirStack endpoint, billed per result returned with a per-run cap. Free credit or trial: Every new workspace starts with a signup Grant of free balance.
Apify Store
Actor is built on TheirStack but bring-your-own TheirStack key, and has only a couple of users; per-event rows show $0.003 per company result. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
