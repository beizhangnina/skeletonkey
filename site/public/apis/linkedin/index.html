Route notes

Apify Store
Apify Actor page for the LinkedIn Profile Scraper + Email showed a pricing badge of from $4.00 / 1,000 profile details. Free credit or trial: Free plan renewing $5/month of prepaid usage credit, plus paid monthly plans ($19 Starter / $199 Scale / $999 Business) that add pay-as-you-go usage.
