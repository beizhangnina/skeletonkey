# Study: what makes these feel high-end (structure/feel only — no assets copied)
Screenshots: study/<site>-hero.png and -scroll.png (Chromium 1440x900, captured 2026-10-25).

- **emergences.ai (primary motion ref)** — Framer + Lenis smooth scroll. Layered cinematic image: a full-bleed painted scene with cut-out foreground layers (trees) that part/parallax on scroll, a canvas sky with twinkling stars and an occasional shooting star, and a huge light-weight condensed serif headline centered over it. Motion is slow, ambient, continuous; nothing bounces. Lesson: depth layers + one slow ambient element + one giant serif line.
- **blockrun.ai** — editorial: classical statue photo duotoned into the brand colour, italic serif headline overlapping a giant outlined word, tiny mono meta labels in the corners. Lesson: image treated as tone, not decoration; micro-labels framing the edges.
- **linear.app** — dark, one sans headline ~64px, everything else tiny and grey; product shot is the hero image. Huge contrast between H1 and body; extreme restraint in colour.
- **vercel.com** — white space as the material; one black geometric mark; thin hairline grid. Lesson: hairlines + whitespace = precision.
- **stripe.com** — one bold gradient image bleeding off-canvas, left-aligned headline with a two-tone (dark/colour) phrase. Lesson: two-tone headline gives a focal phrase.
- **raycast.com** — dark, a single dramatic light-streak image behind a centered headline; spotlight and glow focus the eye.
- **arc.net** — black field, single coloured mark, centered serif-ish headline, one CTA. Pure restraint.
- **rauno.me** — oversized type as layout, one bright shape; type is the image.
- **family.co** — playful illustration, very large rounded headline, generous padding; craft in micro-interactions.
- **novacatapult brand-asset** — system thinking: consistent lockups on paper/black/brand panels, generous card padding, rigorous grid.

Applied to SkeletonKey: one dominant Fraunces headline at dramatic scale (≈7–8× body), two-tone brass italic focal phrase, full-bleed dark cinematic key/door image with an ambient light layer (emergences-style: slow dust motes in the door's light shaft + gentle scroll parallax), mono micro-labels at hero edges, hairline rules and wide section spacing below, cream paper sections in strict rhythm.
